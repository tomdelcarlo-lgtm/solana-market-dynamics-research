# Scientific and engineering limitations

## Prediction and phase space

The historical ML split was stratified random, with repeated test-label reuse during selection. Model scores are saved exploratory outputs, not a reproduced untouched temporal evaluation. Calibration on resampled prevalence and feature-definition differences limit probability interpretation. Original SQL label horizons, future-selection effects and the graduation/migration proxy need to remain qualified.

The five-axis proposal, four-axis checkpoint implementation and derived warehouse phase table are different artifacts. Seven checkpoints are too sparse for formal chaos inference. The warehouse association does not establish added trajectory value beyond simpler baselines. Later chaos work stopped at an incomplete specification gate; it neither proves chaos nor rules out deterministic structure.

## Observation and execution

First observed trade may not be launch. Reconnect gaps, bounded dedup state, timestamp fallback and source-dependent USD conversion affect histories. DDL for observation-time fields does not imply complete populated timing coverage. The inspected normalized database did not evidence raw/snapshot/inference ingestion.

Paper and live summaries differ in periods, policies, size, uptime and eligibility. One paper exit ID repeats. Live accounting is a logged-USD convention with unmatched buys and incomplete fee/capital reconciliation; the older 173–174 headline remains unreconciled. Absolute quote deviation is not signed adverse slippage. Repeated failed SELL rows are not independent failed trades.

BUY timing quantiles describe 39 selected rows and local confirmation boundaries. Complete event propagation, inclusion and SELL latency distributions are unavailable. Neither infrastructure nor market competition is identified as the sole causal explanation of the observed outcomes. No market-wide participant profitability percentages are established.

## Public reproducibility

Aggregate checks are reproducible; the original extractions/model fits/trade joins are not independently reconstructible from this release. Synthetic fixtures are method examples, not market samples. The retained latency histogram cannot be rebuilt from quantiles alone, so it remains a provenance-labeled historical figure. All new SVGs regenerate exactly from included aggregates, specified parameters or conceptual definitions.

## Interpretation of the pause

June 11 documents a particular trading pause with collection retained. The later post-migration checkpoint outcome remains unknown. Opportunity cost is the researcher's qualitative interpretation; no precise lifetime project ROI, hours ledger, probability of success or optimal stopping estimate is claimed.
